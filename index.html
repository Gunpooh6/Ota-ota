Day-by-Day Journey Plan

Dec 13 Sunday
Day 1 • Travel Day

Flight EZY8745 & Resort Check-In

Depart London Gatwick at 12:10 GMT. Land in Hurghada at 19:30 local time. Purchase entry visa ($25 cash), board airport transfer to King Tut Aqua Beach Resort (~18 mins). Enjoy late night buffet dinner and drinks.

Dec 14 Monday
Day 2 • Resort Relax & Pool Orientation

Aqua Park, Beach & Diamond Diving Registration

Relax by the hotel pools and aqua slides. Swim and snorkel off the resort jetty. Check in with Diamond Diving team to confirm registration, paperwork, wetsuit sizes & gear fitting.

Dec 15 Tuesday
Day 3 • Diamond Diving Course Day 1 (~€345)

Theory & Confined Pool Skills

First day of underwater license training with Diamond Diving. Morning classroom theory covering breathing regulator safety, pressure equalization, and pool skills practice in shallow waters.

Dec 16 Wednesday
Day 4 • Diamond Diving Course Day 2

Open Water Reef Dives 1 & 2

Board the Diamond Diving boat from the marina. Complete first two open ocean reef dives at shallow Red Sea coral reefs. Spot marine life and practice underwater buoyancy control.

Dec 17 Thursday
Day 5 • Diamond Diving Course Day 3

Final Dives & License Graduation 🎓

Complete final Open Water Dives 3 & 4 plus navigation test. Pass official Scuba Diving license exam with Diamond Diving! Receive certification logbook card and celebrate at the resort main bar.

Dec 18 Friday
Day 6 • Day of Rest & Resort Snorkeling

Rest Day & Coral Reef Snorkeling

Relaxing rest day after 3 intense diving days. Enjoy the resort jetty coral reef, pools, and all-inclusive buffet lounge.

Dec 19 Saturday
Day 7 • Desert Safari (~£40)

ATV Quad Biking, Camel Ride & Bedouin BBQ

Mid-afternoon pickup for desert safari. Drive ATV quads through desert sand dunes, visit a Bedouin village, enjoy camel riding, sunset over the mountains, and Bedouin BBQ dinner with live show.

Dec 20 Sunday
Day 8 • Departure Day

Flight EZY8744 Back to London Gatwick

Morning hotel check-out. Transfer to Hurghada Airport (HRG) for easyJet flight EZY8744 departing at 17:45. Land at London Gatwick (LGW) at 21:45 GMT.
